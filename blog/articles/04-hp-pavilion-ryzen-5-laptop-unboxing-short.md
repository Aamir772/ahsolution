## Video context: a teaser, not a review

This is a 22-second teaser. The "Coming Soon" title says it plainly: there are no specifications, no port tour and no performance demo, so claiming a configuration from it would not be honest.

The value here is different: the checklist works with any new laptop. The most common mistake with budget and mid-range laptops is to start setup the moment the box opens and skip verification. Follow the steps below in order — these small checks can prevent a replacement later.

On day one the order is: (1) seal and contents, (2) invoice-model match, (3) physical inspection, (4) setup and updates, (5) warranty registration.

## Day one: delivery and box check

As soon as the courier leaves, check the box:

- **Outer carton:** dents, water marks or a torn carton signal transit damage. Take photos — they help with a claim.
- **Seal intact:** a cut or chipped factory seal or HP tape may mean an open-box unit. Ask the seller.
- **Contents count:** laptop, charger (adapter and power cord), quick start guide, warranty card, and the accessory promised in the listing. Check against the invoice.
- **Adapter wattage:** it is printed on the adapter and should match the invoice and listing — an underpowered adapter harms performance and charging.

## Invoice, model number and order match

The box label, the model on the sticker under the laptop and the GST bill should all carry the same model number, and Windows Settings → About or System Information should show the same.

Even if only "HP Pavilion Ryzen 5" is written, the bill carries the full SKU — the RAM, storage and GPU suffixes hide inside that model number. If the listing and bill do not match, raise it in writing within the replacement window.

## Physical inspection: body, hinge, display, ports

Turn it over in good light:

- **Body:** scratches, dents, a loose panel — a new laptop should be flawless.
- **Hinge:** open and close the lid 8–10 times; clicking, a tight spot or wobble signals a problem.
- **Display:** check for dead pixels and backlight bleed on a white or blue wallpaper, and for coating damage.
- **Keyboard and trackpad:** press every key (spot stuck or double-trigger keys) and test the backlight; then the trackpad click, scroll and gestures.
- **Ports:** plug a pen drive into each USB port, and check HDMI output, the audio jack and the charging port fit.
- **Webcam, mic and speakers:** record a video in the camera app and play it back; listen for speaker distortion.

## First boot: setup, bloatware and updates

1. **OOBE:** region, keyboard, network. A Microsoft account is not compulsory — if privacy is the priority, use a local account.
2. **Activation:** Settings → Activation should show "activated". The digital license is linked to the bill, so keep the invoice.
3. **Windows Update:** finish the updates on day one, then the [optional driver updates](https://support.hp.com/us-en/drivers) — display and Wi-Fi problems are often fixed by a driver update.
4. **Preinstalled apps:** uninstall what you do not need, but do not delete the recovery partition or the HP support tools — service may need them.
5. **Disk Management:** the promised SSD and partition should be there; 256GB promised but 128GB showing is a mismatch.
6. **Recovery drive (optional):** creating one on a pen drive is cheap insurance.

## Warranty registration and documents

- Note the **serial number** (box label, bottom sticker, or `wmic bios get serialnumber`).
- Register the product through HP's [warranty support channel](https://support.hp.com/us-en/check-warranty).
- Keep the invoice, a box-label photo and the warranty confirmation in one folder.
- Extended warranty offers often arrive within the first 30 days — read them before deciding.

## Practical checks during the first week

The return window is often short (7–14 days), so test everything inside it.

| Day | What to test |
|---|---|
| Day 1–2 | Body, display, keyboard, ports, webcam/mic, speakers |
| Day 3–4 | Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sleep/wake, lid close, charging cycles |
| Day 5–6 | 30–45 minute sustained load — fan noise and heat baseline |
| Day 7+ | Battery drain pattern, adapter heat, support responsiveness |

Report a defect in writing as soon as you see it; verbal promises do not hold.

## Troubleshooting and limitations

- **Dead pixel policy:** every seller's rule differs, so report inside the return window — a claim gets harder afterwards.
- **Windows update fails:** retry, then use startup repair from recovery; an HP image restore is also an option.
- **Removing bloatware removes functions:** some HP utilities (function keys, battery health) run from a manufacturer app — understand the role before uninstalling.
- **Wi-Fi drops:** update the driver, then test on the router.
- **"Ryzen 5" is a different generation:** the teaser does not confirm the CPU generation — always read System Information.

## Safety and privacy

A delivery-time fraud asks for an OTP: never share an OTP or PIN under "delivery verification" or "payment confirmation". Do not enter personal data on public Wi-Fi during setup, and check the permissions of preinstalled trial apps — basics are in our [guide on choosing apps safely](guides/choose-android-apps-safely.html). Do not post your serial number or bill publicly on social media. Use only official HP channels for warranty claims.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should be checked first while unboxing?

The condition of the seal and packaging, then the list of box contents, then the invoice model number match. These three should be clear in the first 15 minutes, because the return window starts at delivery.

### Why is verifying the charger wattage important?

The adapter has to match the laptop's power demand. An undersized charger charges slowly or causes throttling under load, and a wrong adapter risks overheating. Label, invoice and listing should show the same wattage.

### Can Windows be set up with a local account?

Yes, if the option is available. A Microsoft account syncs your data, but if privacy is the priority a [local account](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/manage/change-from-a-local-account-to-a-microsoft-account-in-windows) is practical, and you can add one later from Settings. Basics are on our [privacy.html](privacy.html) page.

### Does uninstalling preinstalled apps void the warranty?

Usually not — removing apps does not void the hardware warranty. Do not delete the recovery partition, BIOS or support tools, because service may need them. If unsure, confirm with HP support.

### How long is the return window?

It depends on the seller and category — usually a 7–10 day range, counted from the delivery day: physical checks on day one, the stress test by day 3–4, and battery observation inside the window.

## Final recommendations

A teaser creates excitement; a buying decision is built on details. As soon as the laptop arrives, check the seal, box contents, invoice-model match and physical inspection, and only then set up and clear the bloatware. Registering the warranty and noting the serial number takes ten minutes and can save money later. The first week is the real test — observe the hardware checks, updates, battery and fan noise inside the return window, and report any defect in writing. Confirm the final configuration against the official listing and System Information.
