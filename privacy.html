Privacy notice.

A simple explanation of how this website works.

Contact

This website provides links to AH Solution's social profiles and an email link. Clicking the email link opens your email application; the website does not store a contact form submission.

Updates form

The optional updates form on the home page does not send data to a server on this website. It opens your email application with a pre-filled message so you can confirm manually. An email address is only ever used to send occasional updates, and you can opt out at any time.

Third-party services

Videos are embedded from YouTube and only load after you press play; images may load from Unsplash; icons and fonts may load from Google Fonts. Those services may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests their content. Review their own privacy policies for details.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

This version does not include an analytics, advertising, or tracking integration, and it does not set advertising cookies. If these are added later, this notice should be reviewed and updated to describe the services used accurately.

Updates

This page is a plain-language notice, not legal advice. Update it before enabling advertising, analytics, email collection, or any other feature that processes visitor information.
